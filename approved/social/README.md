# Approved social posts

**Committed, unlike `out/`.** This is the durable record of what Giusi actually
approved and what was sent to Make — one `.md` (the captions as approved) and
one `.json` (the payload as sent) per post.

Written by `schedule-social-post`, never by `generate-social-post`: a file here
means a human said yes, not that a draft was produced. Don't hand-edit these to
change copy — the point is that they record what was approved, not what someone
wishes had been. To change a post, redraft it and approve it again.
